Booking & Deposits

  • All sessions must be booked in advance. Inquiries submitted through our form are not confirmed sessions.
  • A non-refundable deposit is required to lock in your session time. No session is confirmed until the deposit is received.
  • Deposits are applied toward your final session total.
  • Remaining balance is due at or before the session begins, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Deposits are non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • Rescheduling requests must be made at least 48 hours before your booked session. Requests made with less notice may result in forfeiture of your deposit.
  • No-shows forfeit the full deposit and any remaining session credit.

Session Policy

  • No unattended sessions. The booking artist must be present for the duration of the session.
  • Sessions begin at the scheduled start time. Late arrivals reduce available session time — the session will still end at the originally scheduled time.
  • Additional time beyond the booked session is subject to availability and additional charges at our standard hourly rate.
  • Guests must be approved in advance. PRXDIGY STUDIO reserves the right to limit the number of guests for any session.
  • Illegal activity, damage to the space, or disruptive behavior will result in immediate termination of the session with no refund.

Revisions

  • Mixing and mastering packages include up to 2 rounds of revisions within 14 days of project delivery, unless otherwise specified in your agreement.
  • Revisions must be submitted in a single, consolidated list per round. Additional revisions or changes requested beyond the included rounds are billed at our standard rate.
  • Major creative direction changes or re-recording requests are considered new work and billed separately.
